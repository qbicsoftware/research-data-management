# Edit your profile

Update your username or link your ORCID account.

## Open your profile

1. Log in to the [Data Manager](https://rdm.qbic.uni-tuebingen.de/login).
2. Click your profile icon (top right) and select **User profile**.

    ![Profile menu](images/edit_user_information/edit_user_information.png){.screenshot}

Your profile page shows your username, full name, email, and profile image.

![Profile page](images/edit_user_information/edit_user_information_profile.png){.screenshot}

## Change your username

1. Click the blue **Change Username** link.
2. Enter your new username.
3. Click **Save**.

The system checks availability. If the name isn't taken, your username updates immediately.

![Updated username](images/edit_user_information/edit_user_information_changed.png){.screenshot}

!!! info "Username visibility"
    Your username is visible to other users on the platform.

## Link ORCID

If you registered with email and want to add ORCID login later:

1. On your profile page, click **Link ORCiD account**.

    ![Link ORCID](images/edit_user_information/edit_user_information_link_orcid_account.png){.screenshot}

2. Sign in to ORCID and click **Authorize access**.

    ![ORCID login](images/edit_user_information/link_orcid_login.png){.screenshot}

    ![Authorise](images/edit_user_information/edit_user_information_orcid_linkage_authorize_access.png){.screenshot}

3. Your linked ORCID appears on your profile page.

    ![ORCID linked](images/edit_user_information/edit_user_information_orcid_account_linked.png){.screenshot}
